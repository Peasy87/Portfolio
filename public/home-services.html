Common questions

Straight answers

What happens in the audit?

We spend about an hour with you, on site or on a call, walking through how calls, quotes, scheduling and paperwork work today. Within a week you get a short report: where you're losing time and jobs, what each leak costs, and what we'd build first. It's yours to keep whether you hire us or not.

Do I have to switch software?

Usually not. We connect the tools you already use, like your calendar, your field-service app and your accounting software. If something truly can't connect, we'll tell you in the audit before you spend anything on a build.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

You decide how it introduces itself. Most owners have it say it's the company's assistant. It handles the basics, books the job, and hands off to you for anything it shouldn't answer.

How fast can this be running?

A single quick-win, like missed-call text-back, is usually live in about two weeks. Larger systems take two to four weeks after the audit.

What if something breaks?

On a Care Plan we monitor everything and fix problems, often before you notice them. Without a plan, fixes are billed by the hour.

Is the audit really credited toward the build?

Yes. If you go ahead with a build, the full audit fee comes off the price.
